# Character presence and quiet busts

The current completed POV message controls which busts remain on stage after a four-beat grace period. A character participates by speaking (including an alias/thought) or through an explicit `#ENTER`. Someone absent from the whole message leaves once they have spent four complete beats without speaking. Explicit exits and scene/location changes still apply immediately.

Each dialogue, narration or artifact beat increases a present character's quiet count unless that character is the current speaker. Silent beats 1–4 keep the normal position, brightness, opacity and saturation. On the fifth silent beat, a participating character begins retreating and dimming over four more beats toward the inactive-brightness setting. Narration's separate global dim still applies. Movement/dimming settles over 1.8 seconds; speaking resets the silence count and restores focus over 0.35 seconds. Commands and repeated renders do not add quiet beats. Backward navigation reconstructs the saved beat's state.

Characters missing from a new message keep any remaining grace beats, then slide fully off their existing side and fade out over 1.4 seconds on the fifth silent beat. Silence carries across responses; a new response does not restart the count or revive an already absent character. The departing image retains its expression; pending image work, breathing and blinking are suspended, and resources are released after departure. A return before the exit finishes reuses the same body and cancels removal. Reduced motion keeps the four-beat grace, then removes the body without animation. Closing or changing worlds cancels exits and releases their resources.

The existing cast-memory window caches recent identities/emotions after departure; a short response window cannot remove a body before its four-beat grace ends. Untagged dialogue keeps a cached expression; explicit `[default]`, `[neutral]` or another emotion replaces it normally. The narrator never receives a body. Missing-art shadows follow the same presence rules, and full-body anchors/character offsets remain unchanged.

Message-extra snapshots retain cached cast entries for compatibility. Each character includes `present` and `silentBeats` (0–8); filter `characters` by `character.present !== false` to obtain the visible cast, including any remaining grace. Cached entries with `present: false` do not occupy visible stage slots or leave stale busts on screen.
